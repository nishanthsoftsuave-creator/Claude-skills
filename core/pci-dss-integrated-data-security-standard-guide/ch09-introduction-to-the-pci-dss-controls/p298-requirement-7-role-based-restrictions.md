# Requirement 7: Role-Based Restrictions
(Source: PDF pp. 298-299)

<!-- PDF p. 298 (book p. 276) -->

Ensure critical data can only be accessed by authorized personnel, systems, and processes based upon the strict need to know/need to access requirements for their role.

This applies to both logical and physical entry control practices. In Figure 9-30, you can see an example of the ANA restricting access to their encampment (Camp Shorabak), which was located within the confines of Camp Bastion. Despite the fact that members of the RAF Police and United States Marine Corps needed to gain access to their enclave, access had to be granted based upon specific role-based requirements.
