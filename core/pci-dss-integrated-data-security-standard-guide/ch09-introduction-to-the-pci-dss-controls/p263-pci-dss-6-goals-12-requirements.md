# PCI DSS: 6 Goals, 12 Requirements
(Source: PDF p. 263)

<!-- PDF p. 263 (book p. 241) -->

As I write this book, the PCI Security Standards Council (PCI SSC) are looking to update the PCI DSS to version 4.0. However, the basic model will remain (6 Goals supported by 12 Requirements). Therefore, rather than delve into the complexities, at the control level, this chapter will provide an overview of the intent of the model, and the readers should familiarize themselves with the specific controls that support their particular requirement by visiting the PCI SSC web site.[^11]
