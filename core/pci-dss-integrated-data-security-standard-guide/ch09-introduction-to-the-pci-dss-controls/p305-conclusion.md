# Conclusion
(Source: PDF pp. 305-306)

<!-- PDF p. 305 (book p. 283) -->

*The PCI DSS Controls Framework has been designed to provide you with an integrated structure, where changes to the earlier requirements could have an impact on the subsequent layers.*

**Changes need to be carefully managed, so as to align with the other dependencies.** *Have you implemented the three Ps, where the Personnel understand their roles and responsibilities and the Policies and Procedures that apply to them?*

*Do you have a layered network? Are your “Firewall Rules” effectively filtering the network traffic? Have you identified and documented the systems that support the payment card operations?*

*Are these systems “locked down” to prevent misuse? Has data retention been limited based upon strict business needs? Is all CHD moving outside the CDE done so under lock and key?*

<!-- PDF p. 306 (book p. 284) -->

*Are all vulnerabilities remediated against, within a timely manner, based upon the potential risk to prevent exploitation by an attacker?*

*Do you have a formal change management process? Is all in-house software developed securely, so as to ensure that known vulnerabilities are not introduced to the environment?*

*Is access strictly limited? Can you easily detect and rapidly respond to the presence of the* **ABNORMA***L? Do your testing activities provide suitable levels of assurance?*
