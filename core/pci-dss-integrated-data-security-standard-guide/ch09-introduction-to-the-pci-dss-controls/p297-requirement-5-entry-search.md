# Requirement 5: Entry Search
(Source: PDF p. 297)

<!-- PDF p. 297 (book p. 275) -->

Think of this like having a digital explosives and ammunitions search (AES) dog asset, seeking to identify and detect suspicious “payloads” on visitors to your establishment *(think effective malware detection software)*. The search asset needs to be in place and able to detect foreign objects, which could do harm to your network, before they are able to initiate. Criminals are constantly evolving the malware strains, so it is essential that the search asset remains updated and is tested to ensure that it remains an effective asset.
