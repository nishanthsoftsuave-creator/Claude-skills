# Requirement 4: Secure in Motion
(Source: PDF pp. 295-296)

<!-- PDF p. 295 (book p. 273) -->

Much like “Cash In Transit,” there will be a need to move CHD from the vault from one secure location to another. Consequently, just like the Banks when they move money, you need to ensure that the CHD remains under lock and key when transiting over public-facing environments. This includes

- Internet
- Mobile (Wireless, Bluetooth, Cellular, Text, Instant Message, Chat, Radio and Satellite comms)

**Figure 9-27.** *Secure Vault*

<!-- PDF p. 296 (book p. 274) -->

- Virtual Private Networks
- Email

When moving your payment card data out into public environments, ensure that it is adequately protected and kept under lock and key *(encrypted)* while being transported.

This will help mitigate the risks presented from your convoys being ambushed while in flight.
