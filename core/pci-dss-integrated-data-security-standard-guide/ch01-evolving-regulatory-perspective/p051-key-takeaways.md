# Key Takeaways
(Source: PDF p. 51)

<!-- PDF p. 51 (book p. 27) -->

*When consumers provide their personal data, in exchange for goods or services, to your data processing operations, it is like they are entrusting their children (or other most valued items) to a taxi, courier, or bus service.*

*Consequently, they quite rightly expect that the drivers will be licensed and safe and the vehicles are well maintained and subject to an annual safety check.*

*The same applies to your personal data processing operations.*

**Maintaining fleet safety requires leadership, support, and teamwork!** *Do you set the “Tone at the Top”? Do you understand your personal data processing operations? Do all personnel understand what “Safe Driving” looks like? Do you have a well-maintained fleet? In the case of an event/incident, do you have sufficient fleet maintenance records? Is Data Protection embedded into your business culture? Do you apply a team effort? Do all personnel receive initial and periodic refresher training? Are your fleet drivers policed? Are your personnel encouraged to report any potential incidents of “Dangerous Driving”?*

*In the event of an accident (incident), do your staff know what to do?*
