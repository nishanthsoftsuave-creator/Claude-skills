# Reality Bites
(Source: PDF p. 82)

<!-- PDF p. 82 (book p. 58) -->

In 1250 BC, the Greeks laid siege on the City of Troy.[^48] For over a decade, the city’s defenses had managed to thwart all the previous attacks. The Trojan defenses were undermined when they took receipt of an unexpected gift (a large wooden horse), which had contained an undetected clandestine payload (Greek Warriors).

The Trojans failed to effectively search and detect the presence of this malicious payload and, as a result, freely wheeled the wooden horse through the various layers of defense, nearer the core of the city.

The same tactics, techniques, and protocols (TTPs) can still be seen used in modern-day cyber-attacks, with Trojan Horse-style malware attacks still being recorded as the most common type of attack[^49] *(along with phishing/spear-phishing, rootkit, and SQL injection)* in 2019.
