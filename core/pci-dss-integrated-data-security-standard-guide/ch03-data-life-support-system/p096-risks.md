# Risks
(Source: PDF p. 96)

<!-- PDF p. 96 (book p. 72) -->

Much like the circulatory system within the human body, business needs to maintain good and healthy data (blood) flows to help maintain the critical processes (major organs).

**Ask yourself:**

- *Do I understand the threats to my circulatory system?*
- *How healthy is my business?*
- *When did I last have a checkup and was this done by someone who understands my business?*
- *Does everyone involved with my circulatory system understand the need to maintain its healthy functions?*
- *What measures do I employ to maintain my health?*
- *Do I have sufficiently trained “First Aiders”?*
