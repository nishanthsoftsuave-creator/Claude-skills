# What are the biggest inhibitors to a successful PCI DSS strategy for the protection of my customer cardholder data?
(Source: PDF p. 512)

<!-- PDF p. 512 (book p. 494) -->

*There are many factors that can impede the effectiveness of your PCI DSS strategy, but my top 5 would be*

1. *A lack of governance*

2. *Poor planning and preparation*

3. *Apathy from within the business*

4. *No security steering committee support*

5. *Poor resource management*
