# Case Study 2: Enhanced PCI DSS Program Through Integration into Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
(Source: PDF pp. 201-212)

<!-- PDF p. 201 (book p. 178) -->

Notwithstanding the PCI DSS compliance program, ABC Corporation have recognized the benefits of incorporating the risk management of their payment card operations into a single, centralized, and cohesive risk management program.

In order to achieve this more effectively, they have implemented the use of a COTS Risk Management product to help them provide an aggregated risk view of the perceived risks to the business and to provide a degree of automation of some of the associated action requirements.

The potential benefits and ROI include

- Help create a more risk-focused culture for the organization
- Provide standardized risk reporting
- Improved focus and perspective on risk
- Support of effective coordination of your regulatory and compliance matters
- Improved efficient use of resources
- Estimated time savings = $36,000 per annum[^32]

Figures 6-8 to 6-25 show an array of interfaces that you might get from an effective and easy-to-use ERM solution.

[^32]: Net Cost of Time Saved (e.g., (1680 hours per annum (10 users) @ $200 per hour (average) – Cost of Investment ($150,000)) / Cost of Investment (e.g., $150,000 per annum) × 100.
