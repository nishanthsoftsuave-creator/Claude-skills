# Maturing the Five Pillars of Defense
(Source: PDF p. 406)

<!-- PDF p. 406 (book p. 385) -->

Throughout my career, I have referenced and used multiple controls frameworks and I am often asked which domains I would choose as my foundation for creating Proactive Defenses. This chapter identifies the areas of any Cyber/InfoSec strategy that I believe any business should be focusing their efforts, so as to make them less of a target for criminals. Each pillar is reliant on the pillar before it to help focus your efforts in improving your ability to identify and respond to the presence of ABNORMAL activities within your corporate environment.
