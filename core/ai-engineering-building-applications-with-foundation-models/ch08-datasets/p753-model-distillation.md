# Model Distillation
(Source: PDF pp. 753-754; the section ends on PDF p. 755, where the text above the "Data Processing" heading, the Nemotron-4 outcome and the Llama 3 remark, belongs to this section)

## What the section covers
Model distillation as a special use of data synthesis in which AI-generated data is not a supplement but a requirement. It defines the technique, gives its traditional purpose and examples (DistilBERT, Alpaca, BuzzFeed), notes the licensing caveat, and then separates distillation proper from other kinds of training on synthetic data (reverse instruction, Nemotron-4) (PDF pp. 753-754). It opens by wrapping up the preceding discussion of generating and evaluating synthetic data and its limits (PDF p. 753).

## Meaning
- Model distillation, also called knowledge distillation, trains a small model (the student) to imitate a larger one (the teacher), citing Hinton et al. (2015). The big model's knowledge is "distilled" into the small one (PDF p. 753).
- Distillation implies that the teacher's performance is the student's gold standard. Not every training run on synthetic data qualifies, because synthetic data can also train a student that ends up bigger and stronger than its teacher (PDF p. 754).

## Why it matters
- The traditional aim is a smaller model for deployment. Serving a large model is resource-intensive, so a smaller and faster student that keeps performance close to the teacher is valuable (PDF p. 753).
- Example: DistilBERT, distilled from BERT, is 40% smaller, keeps 97% of BERT's language comprehension and runs 60% faster (Sanh et al., 2019) (PDF p. 753).
- Cost payoff example: BuzzFeed finetuned a Flan-T5 model with LoRA on examples produced by OpenAI's text-davinci-003 and cut its inference cost by 80%, although the book says it was unclear how well the resulting model performed (2023) (PDF p. 754).

## How it works
- The student can be trained from scratch, as DistilBERT was, or finetuned from an existing pre-trained model, as Alpaca was (PDF p. 753).
- Alpaca: in 2023 Taori et al. finetuned Llama-7B (the 7-billion-parameter Llama) on examples generated by text-davinci-003, a 175-billion-parameter model. The result behaves much like text-davinci-003 while being 4% of the teacher's size (PDF pp. 753-754).
- Synthetic instruction data is commonly combined with adapter-based techniques such as LoRA, as in the BuzzFeed case (PDF p. 754).

## Distillation versus other synthetic-data training
- Model bootstrapping with reverse instruction (Li et al., 2023), covered in the previous section, is one example of synthetic data that is not distillation (PDF p. 754).
- Nemotron-4 is another. NVIDIA researchers first pre-trained a 340B-parameter base model, then finetuned it on instruction and preference data produced by Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1 (Jiang et al., 2024), a 56-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model (PDF p. 754). The resulting student, Nemotron-4-340B-Instruct, beat its teacher on a range of tasks (NVIDIA, 2024) (PDF p. 755). The book's endnote adds that comparing parameter counts of a mixture-of-experts model (Mixtral) with a dense one (Nemotron-4) is not fair, but the teacher is still the smaller model.
- The Llama 3 paper observes that training on data from a more capable model can substantially improve a model, whereas training indiscriminately on its own generated data does not help and can degrade it. By adding mechanisms to check synthetic-data quality and keeping only verified data, the Llama 3 team could keep improving a model with its own generations (PDF p. 755).

## Limitations and failure modes
- Licensing: many model licenses forbid using their outputs to train other models, especially competing ones, so not every model can be distilled (PDF p. 754).
- Unverified self-generated data can degrade a model (PDF p. 755).

## When and how to apply
INTERPRETATION: If the goal is cheaper serving, finetune a small model, possibly with LoRA, on teacher outputs after checking the teacher's license; if the goal is to exceed the teacher, filter the synthetic data through verification as in the Llama 3 account.
