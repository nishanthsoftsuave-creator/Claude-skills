# Epilogue
(Source: PDF pp. 930-931)

## What the section covers
The author's short closing note to the reader.

## Content
- The reader has finished a technical book of more than 150,000 words, 160 illustrations, 250 footnotes, and 975 reference links (PDF p. 930).
- The author expresses gratitude: being able to set aside time to learn is a privilege, and the author is thankful for the chance to write and learn, and to the reader for spending learning time on the book (PDF p. 930).
- A reflection on technical writing: its hardest part is not finding correct answers but asking the right questions. Writing inspired many questions that led to useful and fun discoveries, and the author hopes the book prompted interesting questions for the reader too (PDF p. 930).
- Outlook: many impressive applications already exist on top of foundation models and the number will surely grow exponentially. More systematic approaches to AI engineering, like those in this book, make development easier and so enable even more applications (PDF p. 930).
- Invitation: readers with use cases to discuss may contact the author, who enjoys hearing about interesting problems and solutions, through the social and contact channels the author lists (X, LinkedIn, and a contact page on the author's website) (PDF p. 930).
- Resources: the book's GitHub repository, github.com/chiphuyen/aie-book, has more AI engineering material (PDF p. 931).
- Closing sentiment: AI engineering has many challenges, not all fun, but all are opportunities for growth and impact; the author looks forward to learning what readers will build (PDF p. 931).
