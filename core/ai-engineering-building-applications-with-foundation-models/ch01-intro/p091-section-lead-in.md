# The AI Engineering Stack (Lead-in)
(Source: PDF pp. 91-92)

## What the section covers
The opening of the AI Engineering Stack section: why to focus on fundamental building blocks rather than hype, the fact that AI engineering grew out of ML engineering, how companies label these roles, and the plan to compare them layer by layer. The section opens on PDF p. 91 after the closing sentence of the planning part.

## Why focus on fundamentals
AI engineering's rapid growth brought a lot of hype and fear of missing out (FOMO). New tools, techniques, models and applications appear daily and can overwhelm. The book's answer is to look at the fundamental building blocks instead of chasing "constantly shifting sand" (PDF p. 91).

## Origin in ML engineering
- AI engineering evolved out of ML engineering. When a company starts experimenting with foundation models, its existing ML team naturally leads the effort (PDF p. 91).
- Some companies treat AI engineering as the same as ML engineering. Figure 1-12 (PDF p. 92, rendered) is a set of eight LinkedIn job headlines from December 17, 2023 (caption: many companies put AI engineering and ML engineering under the same umbrella). Titles shown: Senior Machine Learning Engineer, Generative AI (Robinhood); Staff+ Machine Learning Engineer - Generative AI (Handshake); Machine Learning Engineer, Large Language Model & Generative AI (TikTok); Staff Machine Learning Research Engineer, Foundation Models (Scale AI); Software Engineer, AI Systems (Meta); ML/AI Operations Engineer (Gusto); AI/ML Staff Software Engineer (Apptio); and Principal Machine Learning Engineer (GoFundMe), whose listing snippet says some exposure to LLMs, via the OpenAI API or open source models and frameworks such as Hugging Face, LangChain, Llama-2, Falcon or Dolly, is a plus. In each case generative AI or LLM work sits under an ML or software engineering title.
- Other companies write separate job descriptions for AI engineering, shown in Figure 1-13, which is printed on the next page and described in C01-020.

## Overlap of the roles
Wherever organizations place AI and ML engineers, the roles overlap significantly. Existing ML engineers can add AI engineering to their skills to widen job prospects. There are also AI engineers who have no prior ML experience (PDF p. 92).
