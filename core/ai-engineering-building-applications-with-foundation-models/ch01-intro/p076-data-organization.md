# Data Organization
(Source: PDF p. 76; the section's closing sentences run onto the next page, which the matrix assigns to C01-013)

## What the section covers
How AI helps organize the growing volume of unstructured and semistructured data so it can be searched later.

## Why it matters
- Information aggregation goes hand in hand with data organization: the more information gathered, the more important it is to organize it (PDF p. 76).
- Data volume will keep growing. Smartphone users keep taking photos and videos, companies log everything about products, employees and customers, and billions of contracts are created every year (PDF p. 76).
- Photos, videos, logs and PDFs are unstructured or semistructured. The author calls it essential to organize them so they can be searched later (PDF p. 76).

## How AI helps
- AI can automatically write text descriptions of images and videos, and can match text queries to visuals that fit. Google Photos already surfaces images that match search queries (PDF p. 76).
- Google Image Search goes further: if no existing image meets the user's need, it can generate one (PDF p. 76).
- AI is very good at data analysis. It can write programs that produce data visualizations, identify outliers, and make predictions such as revenue forecasts (PDF p. 76).
- Enterprises can use AI to extract structured information from unstructured data, which both organizes the data and aids search (PDF p. 76). Examples of such extraction continue in C01-013's range.
