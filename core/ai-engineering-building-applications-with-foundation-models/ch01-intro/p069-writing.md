# Use Case: Writing
(Source: PDF pp. 69-70, tail PDF p. 71)

## What the section covers
Why writing is an ideal AI application, an MIT experiment quantifying ChatGPT's effect, consumer and enterprise uses, AI-generated books, and the downside of abuse, including junk guidebooks and SEO-driven content farms.

## Why writing is ideal
- AI has long aided writing: autocorrect and auto-completion on smartphones are AI-powered (PDF p. 69).
- Three reasons it fits: we write a lot, writing can be tedious, and we have a high tolerance for mistakes, since a suggestion you dislike can simply be ignored (PDF p. 69).
- It is unsurprising that LLMs write well, because they are trained for text completion (PDF p. 69).

## The MIT study (Noy and Zhang, 2023) (PDF p. 69)
- Occupation-specific writing tasks were assigned to 453 college-educated professionals, with a random half exposed to ChatGPT.
- Exposed group: average time dropped 40% and output quality rose 18%.
- ChatGPT narrowed the quality gap between workers, so it helps more those with less inclination for writing.
- Exposed workers were 2 times as likely to report using ChatGPT in their real job two weeks later, and 1.6 times as likely two months after that.

## Consumer uses (PDF pp. 69-70)
- People use AI to communicate better: turn an angry email into a pleasant one, expand bullet points into paragraphs. Several people said they no longer send an important email without having AI improve it (PDF p. 69).
- Students use AI to write essays; writers use it to write books. Many startups generate children's, fan fiction, romance and fantasy books (PDF p. 70).
- AI books can be interactive: the plot can change with the reader's preferences, so readers help create the story. One children's reading app finds the words a child struggles with and generates stories built around them (PDF p. 70).
- Google Docs, Notion and Gmail use AI to improve writing. Grammarly finetunes a model to make writing more fluent, coherent and clear (PDF p. 70).

## Enterprise uses (PDF p. 70)
- Common in sales, marketing and team communication. Managers told the author they use AI to help write performance reports.
- It helps craft cold outreach emails, ad copy and product descriptions. CRM apps such as HubSpot and Salesforce offer tools to generate web content and outreach emails.

## Abuse and failure modes
- In 2023 the New York Times reported Amazon being flooded with shoddy AI-generated travel guidebooks, each with an AI-generated author bio, website and rave reviews (PDF p. 70).
- AI seems especially good at SEO, perhaps because models are trained on internet data full of SEO-optimized text (PDF p. 71).
- That skill has enabled a new generation of content farms: junk websites filled with AI content to rank highly on Google and drive traffic, then monetized by selling ad spots through ad exchanges (PDF p. 71).
- In June 2023 NewsGuard identified almost 400 ads from 141 popular brands on junk AI-generated sites; one such site published 1,200 articles a day (PDF p. 71).
- The author warns that unless this is curtailed, future internet content will be AI-generated, which the author calls "pretty bleak" (PDF p. 71).

## Time-sensitive statements
The study results (2023), NewsGuard counts (June 2023) and the Amazon guidebook report (2023) are dated.
