# Conversational Bots
(Source: PDF p. 73, continuing on the tail page PDF p. 74)

## What the section covers
The foundation-model use case of conversational bots: what they are used for by consumers, researchers and enterprises, and the interfaces (text, voice, 3D) through which they appear. The top of PDF p. 73 belongs to the preceding Education section (C01-009) and is not repeated here.

## Meaning
Conversational bots are described as versatile. They help people find information, explain concepts and brainstorm ideas (PDF p. 73).

## Consumer and research uses
- Beyond information help, a bot can act as a companion or a therapist, and can emulate personalities so that a person can talk to a digital copy of anyone they like (PDF p. 73).
- Digital girlfriends and boyfriends became popular in a very short time. The author notes that many people already spend more time talking to bots than to humans, and that some worry AI will damage dating (PDF p. 73).
- In research, a group of conversational bots can be used to simulate a society, which lets researchers study social dynamics (Park et al., 2023) (PDF p. 73).

## Enterprise uses
- The most popular enterprise bots are customer support bots. They can cut costs and improve customer experience because they answer sooner than human agents (PDF p. 74).
- AI can also serve as a product copilot that walks customers through painful or confusing tasks, with the examples of filing insurance claims, doing taxes, and looking up corporate policies (PDF p. 74).

## Interfaces beyond text
- ChatGPT's success set off a wave of text-based bots, but text is not the only interface. Voice assistants (Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa) have existed for years, and 3D conversational bots are already common in games and gaining ground in retail and marketing (PDF p. 74).
- A notable 3D use case is the smart NPC (non-player character); NVIDIA demos from Inworld and Convai are cited. NPCs matter for advancing a game's storyline, but without AI they are scripted to do simple actions with limited dialogue. AI can make them much smarter, changing the dynamics of existing games such as The Sims and Skyrim and enabling games that were not possible before (PDF p. 74).

## Why it matters
The section shows the breadth of the conversational pattern: the same core capability serves cost reduction (support), guided task completion (copilots), entertainment (NPCs), companionship and research simulation.

## Time-sensitive statements
Named products and demos (ChatGPT, Siri, Alexa, Inworld, Convai, The Sims, Skyrim) and the claim about the popularity of digital partners reflect the state of the market at the time of writing.
