# Use Case: Education
(Source: PDF pp. 71-72, tail PDF p. 73)

## What the section covers
How schools reacted to ChatGPT, how AI can personalize learning, specific teaching uses (language practice, quizzes, debate partner, teaching assistants), the Duolingo course-creation finding, and the double-edged effect on education businesses.

## Initial reaction (PDF p. 71)
- When ChatGPT is down, OpenAI's Discord is flooded with students complaining they cannot finish homework.
- Several education boards, including New York City Public Schools and the Los Angeles Unified School District, banned ChatGPT for fear of cheating, but reversed within a few months.
- The author's stance: rather than banning AI, schools could use it to help students learn faster.

## Personalization (PDF pp. 71-72)
- AI can summarize textbooks and generate personalized lecture plans per student. The author finds it strange that ads are personalized while education is not, given that everyone is different (PDF p. 71).
- AI can adapt materials to the format that suits each learner. Examples: auditory learners can have materials read aloud; students who love animals can have visualizations adapted to feature more animals; those who find code easier than math equations can have equations translated into code (PDF pp. 71-72).

## Language learning and course creation (PDF p. 72)
- AI is especially useful for language learning because you can ask it to roleplay different practice scenarios.
- Pajak and Bicknell (Duolingo, 2022) split course creation into four stages: curriculum design, raw content creation, exercise creation and lesson personalization. AI can help in all four, and personalization benefits most. Figure 1-10 (rendered and read) shows human work shrinking and AI work growing across the stages, from mostly human at curriculum design to mostly AI at personalization.

## Other teaching uses (PDF p. 72)
- Generating quizzes, both multiple-choice and open-ended, and evaluating answers.
- Acting as a debate partner, since AI presents different views on a topic better than the average human.
- Khan Academy offers AI-powered teaching assistants for students and course assistants for teachers.
- A teaching method the author has seen: teachers assign AI-generated essays and students find and correct the mistakes.

## Threat and opportunity (PDF p. 73)
- While many education companies embrace AI, many find AI taking their business. Chegg, which helps students with homework, saw its share price fall from $28 when ChatGPT launched in November 2022 to $2 in September 2024 as students turned to AI.
- The risk is that AI can replace many skills; the opportunity is that AI can tutor you in any skill. For many skills, AI can get someone up to speed quickly, after which they can keep learning on their own and become better than AI.

## Time-sensitive statements
Chegg share prices (November 2022 to September 2024), the school bans and reversals, and Duolingo's 2022 findings are dated.
