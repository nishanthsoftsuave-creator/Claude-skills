# Information Aggregation
(Source: PDF pp. 74-75)

## What the section covers
Using AI to filter, digest and summarize information, for individual consumers and for enterprises. The section starts partway down PDF p. 74, after the heading; the conversational-bot text above it belongs to C01-010.

## Meaning
The motivating idea is that success depends on one's ability to filter and digest useful information, yet keeping up with emails, Slack messages and news can be overwhelming. AI has proven capable of aggregating and summarizing information (PDF pp. 74-75).

## Evidence cited
According to Salesforce's 2023 Generative AI Snapshot Research, 74% of generative AI users use it to distill complex ideas and summarize information (PDF p. 75).

## Consumer applications
- Many applications process a user's documents (contracts, disclosures, papers) and let the user retrieve information conversationally. This pattern is called talk-to-your-docs (PDF p. 75).
- AI can summarize websites and research and create reports on chosen topics. The author reports finding AI useful for summarizing and comparing papers while writing the book (PDF p. 75).

## Enterprise applications
- Better aggregation and distillation of information can make an organization leaner, because it lowers the burden on middle management (PDF p. 75).
- Example: when Instacart launched an internal prompt marketplace, one of its most popular templates was "Fast Breakdown". It asks the AI to summarize meeting notes, emails and Slack conversations into facts, open questions and action items. Those action items can then be inserted automatically into a project tracking tool and assigned to the right owners (PDF p. 75).
- AI can also surface critical information about potential customers and run analyses of competitors (PDF p. 75).

## When and how to apply
INTERPRETATION: the Instacart example suggests a pattern of a fixed output structure (facts, open questions, action items) plus a downstream integration, so that summaries trigger work rather than only being read.

## Time-sensitive statements
The 74% figure comes from a 2023 survey, and the named companies and tools reflect the time of writing.

## Relations
Follows conversational bots (C01-010). The author notes that more gathered information makes organizing it more important, leading to data organization (C01-012).
