# Use Case: Image and Video Production
(Source: PDF pp. 67-68)

## What the section covers
Why AI suits creative work, the commercial traction of image and video startups, the shift in attitudes toward AI-generated profile pictures, and enterprise use in advertising and marketing.

## Why AI fits creative tasks
- Thanks to its probabilistic nature, AI is great for creative tasks (PDF p. 67). The book points to Chapter 2 for a fuller discussion of that probabilistic nature (PDF p. 68).

## Commercial traction (PDF pp. 67-68)
- Some of the most successful AI startups are creative applications: Midjourney (image generation), Adobe Firefly (photo editing), and Runway, Pika Labs and Sora (video generation).
- In late 2023, at one and a half years old, Midjourney had already generated $200 million in annual recurring revenue.
- As of December 2023, half of the top 10 free Graphics & Design apps on the Apple App Store had AI in their names. The author expects that graphics and design apps will soon include AI by default and no longer need "AI" in the name.

## Consumers: AI profile pictures (PDF p. 68)
- Using AI to generate profile pictures for social media, from LinkedIn to TikTok, is now common. Many job candidates believe AI-generated headshots help them put their best foot forward and improve their odds of a job.
- Perception shifted: in 2019 Facebook banned accounts that used AI-generated profile photos, for safety reasons; by 2023 many social apps offered tools to generate them.

## Enterprises: ads and marketing (PDF p. 68)
- Ads and marketing were quick to adopt AI. It can generate promotional images and videos directly, help brainstorm ideas, or produce first drafts for human experts to iterate on.
- You can generate many ad variants and test which works best for an audience.
- AI can adapt ads by season and location, for example changing leaf colors for fall or adding snow to the ground for winter.

## When and how to apply
INTERPRETATION: the pattern the book describes is AI producing drafts and variants at volume, with humans iterating and with testing choosing winners.

## Time-sensitive statements
Revenue figures, App Store rankings (December 2023) and the attitude shift on AI headshots (2019 versus 2023) are time-bound.
