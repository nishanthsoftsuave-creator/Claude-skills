# Preface - Prerequisite Knowledge and Learning Goals
(Source: PDF p. 17, with the end of the section on PDF p. 18)

## What the section covers
The opening of the preface and the first heading under it. It says what the preface is for, what the book teaches, and what the reader should be able to do after finishing it.

## Meaning: purpose of the preface
- The preface states the foundations a reader needs, the learning goals, and an "archetypical reader" profile, so a prospective reader can judge whether the book will help them (PDF p. 17).
- Readers who are unsure whether the book suits them, or whether their skills are ready for the technical chapters, are told to finish the preface before starting chapter 1 (PDF p. 17).

## What the book teaches (PDF p. 17)
- The book has two sides. It helps the reader defend a web application against hackers, and it also walks through the steps hackers take to investigate and break into one.
- It describes techniques in use today against web applications run by corporations, governments and occasionally hobbyists.
- After those techniques, it turns to securing web applications against the same hackers.
- Along the way the reader is promised new ways of thinking about application architecture, how to integrate security best practices into an engineering organisation, and an evaluation of techniques for defending against the most common and most dangerous attacks.

## Outcomes the author promises
- Reconnaissance: the reader will be able to run recon techniques against applications they have no code-level access to (PDF p. 17).
- Offence: the reader will be able to identify threat vectors and vulnerabilities, and craft payloads that compromise application data, interrupt execution flow or interfere with how the application is meant to work (PDF p. 18).
- Defence: combined with the closing section on securing applications, the reader will be able to spot risky areas of a codebase and write code that defends against attacks that would otherwise leave the application and its users at risk (PDF p. 18).

## Note on progression
The book builds up gradually. A reader who skips ahead and finds missing prerequisites is advised to go back a few chapters. Topics that are not listed as prerequisites in the preface are meant to be explained before they are used (PDF p. 18).
