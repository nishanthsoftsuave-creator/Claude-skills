# Preface - Summary
(Source: PDF p. 30, with the closing paragraph on PDF p. 31)

## What the section covers
A short wrap-up of the preface that restates the book's purpose, who it serves, and how to read it.

## Main points
- The book is multifaceted: it is meant to help readers with offensive interests and readers with defensive interests, and to stay accessible to any developer or administrator whose web programming background spans both client and server (PDF p. 30).
- It walks through techniques that skilled hackers and bug bounty hunters use to break into applications, then teaches the techniques and processes a reader can build into their own software to protect against such hackers (PDF p. 30).
- It is designed to be read cover to cover but can double as an on-demand reference for particular recon techniques, attacks and defences against those attacks (PDF p. 30).
- The overall aim is to make the reader better at web application security in a way that is practical, hands-on and logically progressive, so that no significant prior security experience is needed (PDF p. 30).

## Closing note
The author hopes the hundreds of hours spent writing are useful, that readers pick up something interesting, and invites feedback for future editions (PDF p. 31). The typographic conventions that follow on that page are administrative and have no module.
