# Applied Recon and Offense Techniques
(Source: PDF p. 224)

## What the section covers
How the recon and offense material of the earlier parts supports the defensive work in Part III, and what the book does and does not claim to be.

## Meaning
- Parts I and II are not required before Part III, but deep knowledge of recon and offense gives insight into building stronger defenses that could not be had otherwise (PDF p. 224).

## How recon helps defenders
- Recon techniques show how to camouflage an application from unwanted eyes.
- They also guide the order in which to fix things, because some vulnerabilities are easier for an outsider to find than others (PDF p. 224).

## How offense knowledge helps defenders
- Understanding the common vulnerabilities hackers look for shows what defenses can be raised against them.
- Knowing categories of exploit helps rank fixes, because it shows what type of data is put at risk if one of those exploits is found in the application (PDF p. 224).

## Scope of the book
- The author states the book is not a comprehensive know-all reference. It offers enough foundation to look up more on any of the three areas: recon, offense and defense.
- Completing all three parts should give the vocabulary to communicate about recon techniques, vulnerabilities and mitigations, speed up further learning, and help the reader steer self-study into whichever security area they want to master (PDF p. 224).

## When and how to apply
INTERPRETATION: before ranking fixes, run a small recon pass against your own application and fix what the pass finds easily first.
