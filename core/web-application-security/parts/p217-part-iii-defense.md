# Part III Opener: Defense
(Source: PDF pp. 217-218)

## What the section covers
The short opener to the final part of the book. It states what Part III will do, which defensive techniques it will teach, and what the reader will have gained by the end of the three parts. The second page of the range carries no text.

## Meaning and purpose of the part
- Part III is the last part of the book and is built on the two before it (PDF p. 217).
- It takes a close look at everything that goes into building a modern, full stack web application, and at each point of that analysis it stops to name the significant security risks and concerns.
- After naming a concern, it looks at alternative implementations and at mitigations that reduce the risk (PDF p. 217).

## What the reader will learn
- Techniques that can be folded into a software development life cycle so that fewer vulnerabilities reach production code (PDF p. 217).
- The range of techniques is wide: secure-by-default application architecture, avoiding insecure anti-patterns, security-oriented code review technique, and countermeasures aimed at specific types of exploit.

## How the part is framed
- By the end of the part the reader is expected to hold a foundation in three things: web application reconnaissance, offensive pen-testing techniques, and secure software development (PDF p. 217).
- The author invites readers to reread points of interest in the first two parts, now with added context, or to apply the new skills in the real world.
- The opener closes by announcing the move into Part III and its aim of teaching the skills needed to build hacker-resistant web applications.

## When and how to apply
INTERPRETATION: treat the opener as the statement of intent for the defensive modules. The chapters that follow each pair a class of attack from Part II with its mitigations, so use them together with the matching offense chapters.

## Relations
The opener leads into the chapter on securing modern web applications (WAS-C17-001), then architecture (WAS-C18-001) and code review (WAS-C19-001).
