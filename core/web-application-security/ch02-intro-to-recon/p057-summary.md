# Chapter 2 Summary - Introduction to Web Application Reconnaissance
(Source: PDF p. 57; PDF p. 58 is a blank page)

## What the section covers
The short closing of chapter 2, which restates why recon matters and how its results should be recorded.

## Main points
- Recon techniques are valuable because they build a deep understanding of the technology and structure of a web application and of the services behind it (PDF p. 57).
- Performing recon is not enough on its own: the findings must also be documented in a way that is organised enough to traverse easily at a later date (PDF p. 57).
- The JSON-like notes shown in this chapter are the style the author prefers when documenting recon against a web application (PDF p. 57).
- The most important property of recon note-taking is to keep relationships and hierarchies intact while staying easy to read and to traverse by hand (PDF p. 57).
- Readers must find a documentation style that works for them and scales from small to large applications. If a different style or format suits them better, they should use it, because the content and structure of the notes matter far more than the application or format they are stored in (PDF p. 57).
