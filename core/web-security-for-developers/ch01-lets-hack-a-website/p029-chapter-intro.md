# Chapter 1 Introduction - Let's Hack a Website
(Source: PDF p. 29. The heading "Software Exploits and the Dark Web" starts lower on the same page and is covered in WS-C01-002.)

## What the section covers
The framing paragraph at the start of Chapter 1: the book will teach the security knowledge an effective web developer needs, but first the reader tries seeing a website the way an attacker does (PDF p. 29).

## Meaning
- The exercise is to stand in the adversary's shoes, in order to understand what defenders are up against (PDF p. 29).
- The chapter promises to show how hackers operate and how easy it is to begin hacking (PDF p. 29).

## Why it matters
INTERPRETATION: Starting from the attacker's side establishes the threat model before any defensive material: defenders need an honest picture of how low the barrier to attacking a site is (PDF p. 29).

Followed by the discussion of exploits and black markets in WS-C01-002 and the hands-on walk-through of attack tooling in WS-C01-003. The chapter is listed in the book's roadmap in WS-I-004.
