# Internal HR policy assistant: project brief

Nothing is built yet. This folder only holds the brief.

- Who: about 2,000 employees across two offices; staff write in English or Arabic.
- What: answer questions about HR policies (leave, expenses, benefits) from about 400 policy documents
  kept in SharePoint, updated monthly.
- Constraints: answers within 5 seconds; budget about 2,000 USD per month for model and infrastructure;
  documents contain some salary band tables that most employees must not see; the company prefers
  not to send confidential documents to outside providers unless there is no alternative.
- Team: two backend engineers, no ML specialist; first version wanted in 8 weeks.
- Wrong answers about leave or pay cause real problems and HR escalations.
